Why we’re asking

The live stream of the Music Jam presentations had some major technical difficulties, Glenn and Alex G did all they could and but our internet service provider really shit the bed.

Because of that I feel it's even more important to do a cool recap video, and I would really appreciate your help!

What we need from you

Record a short video of yourself answering the 3 questions below.

Answer these 3 questions

  1. What theme did you get, and how did it inspire your track?
  2. What was fun or interesting about making your track for the jam?
  3. What do you like about being part of events like this with Louisville Makes Games?

Recording checklist

  • Keep it about 30–90 seconds total.
  • A phone video is totally fine.
  • Back camera preferred.
  • Horizontal video preferred.
  • Try to record in a quiet place.
  • Use good lighting.
  • Speak in full sentences.
