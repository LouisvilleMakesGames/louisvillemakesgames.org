# LMG Phone

The LMG phone is an Android phone logged into official LMG social media accounts.

## Device details

- Model: OnePlus 5
- Color: black phone with a round black LMG sticker on the back
- Passcode: 1337
- Charger type: USB Type-C

## Access policy

- Primary users: Directors, Staff, and Volunteers.
- Any community member can request to make a post if they are in Warp Zone Louisville or at an LMG event and have a good social post idea, they just gotta ask a Director, Staff, or Volunteer.

## Accounts on this phone

- Instagram
- Bluesky
- Mastodon
- Facebook
- Meetup

## Community post request flow

1. Got a cool post idea while you are at Warp Zone or an event? Just ask a Director, Staff member, or Volunteer.
2. Keep it simple and share the basics: what it is, where it is happening, and why people should care.
3. We will either post it right away or queue it up for later.

## Guardrails

- Keep account credentials private.
- Log out only with Director/Staff approval.
- Avoid posting personal information without permission.
- Keep content aligned with the LMG [Code of Conduct](https://lmg.cool/code-of-conduct/) and event context.

## Storage and travel policy

- The phone should remain in the Warp Zone Louisville space unless there is an external event.
- When not in use, place it on top of the Game Boy decal cabinet.
